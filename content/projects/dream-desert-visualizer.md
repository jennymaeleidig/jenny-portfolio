# Dream Desert Visualizer

A visualizer for [夢の砂漠 (_dream desert_)](https://geometriclullaby.bandcamp.com/album/desert-sand-feels-warm-at-night) by desert sand feels warm at night and Secret Schools.

It began as a Processing sketch and I ported it to p5.js so it could live on the web.
