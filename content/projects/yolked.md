# Yolked

A virtual desktop toy made for the May 2024 Goblin Bunker Game Jam with [Evan Sooklal](https://www.lazysundays.net/authors/evan-sooklal) doing the art and the blurb and [Devin Toms](https://www.lazysundays.net/authors/devin-toms) on additional code. It was my first game jam, and my first time in Godot.
